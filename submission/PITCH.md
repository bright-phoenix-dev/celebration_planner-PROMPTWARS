# Celebration & Event Planner — The Pitch

Planning major milestone events—weddings, milestone birthdays, and community festivals—is notoriously overwhelming. Hosts struggle with fragmented spreadsheets, opaque vendor costs, unrealistic deadlines, and generic inspiration, resulting in widespread budget blowouts and decision fatigue.

Celebration & Event Planner transforms chaotic event logistics into an intelligent, accessible command center for everyday hosts and community organizers. Unlike static templates or bloated enterprise software, our full-stack GenAI assistant instantly generates backward-planned milestone timelines, dynamic category-wise budget allocations tailored to event typology, and structured vendor checklists with real-time spend tracking. When creative inspiration stalls, our secure Gemini-powered engine produces hyper-personalized, culturally attuned concepts across themes, decor, curated menus, activities, and invitations.

Engineered with strict TypeScript, zero-knowledge local persistence via IndexedDB, robust prompt-injection defenses, and certified WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, Celebration Planner proves modern AI applications can be celebratory, rock-solid, and private by default.
